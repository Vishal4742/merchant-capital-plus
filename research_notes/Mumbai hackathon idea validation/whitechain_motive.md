# Whitechain: motive, mission, what they want (researched 2026-11-05 17:15 IST)

## Core motive: "distribution-first, exchange-powered" Ethereum L2
- Relaunched Aug 18 2026 on the OP Stack, WBT as gas. The press release's thesis: after a product is built, the hard part is users, liquidity and sustained activity. Other L2s compete on performance; Whitechain competes on **distribution**. ([The Block press release](https://www.theblock.co/press-releases/412097/whitechain-relaunches-as-w-groups-distribution-first-ethereum-layer-2))
- Founder quote (Volodymyr Nosov, W Group / WhiteBIT): "There are many strong ecosystems, but what increasingly sets them apart is their ability to distribute" and "great technology does not automatically translate into adoption." (same source)
- Pitch to builders: access to the WhiteBIT exchange (10M+ users) and W Group (40M+ users). ([whitechain.io](https://whitechain.io))

## Taglines (whitechain.io)
"Next Step Onchain." · "Everything you need, already in one place." · "If you can build on Ethereum, you are already a Whitechain builder." · "Early builders don't just join an ecosystem – they define it."

## Who it's for and the focus areas (whitechain.io)
End users on WhiteBIT/W Group (bridge, swap, stake WBT); developers (Builders Program); partners/VCs; **enterprises: B2B payment rails, white-label, "compliant on-chain infrastructure"**; **RWA issuers: tokenization tooling + investor access**. The strongest emphasis is the CEX connection.

## Roadmap (whitechain.io)
- Shipped: OP Stack devnet, testnet 1874, Builders Program, AI developer tooling, explorer + bridge.
- Q4 2026 target: public mainnet, "unified distribution platform", WBT balance continuity, **"genesis dApp ecosystem" on day one**, "Rails" (no details).
- 2027+: distribution; ecosystem & DeFi; institutional & B2B rails; RWA tokenization.
- Planned day-1 primitives: **native DEX, oracle, bridge** ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/press-releases/412097/whitechain-relaunches-as-w-groups-distribution-first-ethereum-layer-2)).

## Money for builders
Builder Program: up to $300k per early-stage project, milestone-based, vesting; Strategic Ecosystem Deals for established protocols; chain expansion support for multichain protocols ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/375870), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/press-releases/412097/whitechain-relaunches-as-w-groups-distribution-first-ethereum-layer-2)).

## WhiteBIT's mission
"Making blockchain technology accessible to everyone in everyday life" (via search summary; the legacy L1 mission was mass adoption through everyday financial operations).

## What this means for a hackathon build (our inference)
They want apps that **bring exchange users on-chain and keep them active**: invisible onboarding (no seed phrase, no gas, which matches the Scale track), everyday payments and B2B rails (Operate), RWA (Tokenize), and genesis-ecosystem apps for day one. Their own words make the bar "will real WhiteBIT users actually use this?", not raw technical novelty.
