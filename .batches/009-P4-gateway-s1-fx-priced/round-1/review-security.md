## What is verified OK

1. **S1 is closed.** PoC 1, the batch-008 sequence, against the new code: `claim: 50000000n | re-apply ONE mid-relay: { ok: false, reason: 'This checkout is being paid.' }`, then `confirm(s1, claimed): { … code: 'HALF' } | HALF on s2: { ok: false, reason: 'That code has been used up.' }`.
   - **Race guard.** The upsert's own guard (`vouchers.ts:215`, `frozen_at IS NULL`) catches a claim that commits between the pre-check (`:186-187`) and the upsert. `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE` locks the conflicting row and tests its `WHERE` against the latest committed version, even under READ COMMITTED, so the upsert returns no row. The reason shown is "used up" rather than "being paid", but the apply is refused.
   - **Opposite order.** If the switch commits first, the claim freezes the new hold and returns its price. The payer's signature then fails verification (see F1/F2 for the release that must follow).
   - **Idempotent claim.** `COALESCE(frozen_at, $2)` keeps the first freeze time.
   - **Counting.** A frozen hold counts in the live-holds subquery whatever its age (`:211`), and `amountDue` reports it (`:234`).
2. **S2 is closed.**
   - `HOLD_MS` is now 45 min (`:47`).
   - The pay route already refuses `validBefore > session.expiresAt` (`pay/route.ts:52`), so no sale signature can settle after the session ends: at most 30 min after the checkout starts, and 10 min for fiat.
   - A claimed (frozen) hold counts until it is confirmed in any case.
3. **S3's date rule is closed.**
   - `usdRate` runs `freshDate(rate.rateAt, now)` again on every call (`quotes.ts:107`). The stale-cache fallback (`:88`) and `createQuote` (`:120`) are covered too.
   - The test serves a cached rate exactly 7 days old, then fails the source, then asks a day later: 503.
4. **FX bands** (`quotes.ts:21-27`).
   - The numbers match ±15% of the 06 Nov rates: EUR 0.88875 → 0.7554/1.0221, GBP 0.74225 → 0.6309/0.8536, SGD 1.2774 → 1.0858/1.4690, INR 94.5 → 80.33/108.68, and AED at its 3.6725 peg ± 1%.
   - Flipped rates are now all refused: EUR 1.1252, GBP 1.3473, SGD 0.7828, INR 0.0106.
   - Rows are still checked as in batch 008: the source URL is a constant, `base` must be USD, the quote must be on the allowlist, the rate must be a plain decimal, and the date must be a real day.
5. **Priced link and page sessions** (`links.ts:76-94`).
   - **USDT `amount` comes only from stored prices.** It is `Σ prices[i] × qty`, where `prices` is `inUsdt` of the stored link price or page menu price. The exceptions are by design: the payer's own amount on an open link, and on an open page, kept within the page's min/max (`pages.ts:135-140`). Quantities are 1..100. The same value goes on the checkout row (`:95`), and vouchers discount off that row.
   - **Line items in the shop's currency can't be abused as USDT.** This is the same convention the shop's own `POST /api/v1/sessions` already uses: items in the checkout currency, checked against the fiat `amount` (`sessions/route.ts:75-92`).
   - No consumer sums items as USDT. `GET /api/v1/sessions/:id` and the webhook `data` (`events.ts:16-29`) leave items out, and the checkout page labels each price with `priced.currency` (`checkout/[sessionId]/page.tsx:49`).
   - The signed amount is always rebuilt server-side from `session.amount` (`pay/route.ts:34`), so an item price can't reach the signature.
   - TTLs: 10 min for fiat and 30 min for USD and USDT, the same rule as the shop route.
6. **Migration 047:** `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS frozen_at BIGINT`, idempotent and ADD-only.
   - No PII; nothing new goes on chain or to Arkiv.
   - Only `lib/server` changed, so nothing new reaches a client bundle.
   - No relayer change.
